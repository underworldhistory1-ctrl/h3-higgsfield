Reference library

Name each asset, then type @name in the prompt. Each asset is bound to its H3 reference input.

Video references guide a new generation. “Whole scene” asks H3 to follow the full clip while changing the detail in your prompt, but cannot preserve every other frame or pixel. For a multi-panel shot sheet, select Storyboard and mention it in the prompt. Up to 9 images, 3 videos, 3 audio references and 12 files total. A selected video soundtrack counts as audio. Each video/audio: 2–15 s; each type totals 15 s. Trim longer clips below; oversized video can fit within 1920×1080. Frame rates convert to 24 fps after upload.
Upload fitting caps the long edge at 2048 px. Native Max caps the short edge at 2048 px, never upscales, and avoids the extra downscale used by Match. It can consume more memory; it cannot recover lost source detail.
